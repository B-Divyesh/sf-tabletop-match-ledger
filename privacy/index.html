Privacy, by keeping score locally.

Tabletop Match Ledger is designed so your players, scores, notes, and match history stay in your browser’s IndexedDB on the device you use. We do not operate an account system, analytics tracker, advertising service, or cloud score database.

Data on your device

The app stores the current match and preferences locally so they survive refreshes and offline play. You can export the ledger as JSON or CSV and clear it by starting a new match. Clearing site data in your browser also removes it.

Purchase and license data

If you buy the optional Table Keeper unlock, checkout is provided by Sociobot/Dodo as merchant of record. The app stores your license token and last verification result in local storage. It sends that token to api.sociobot.in at most once per day to confirm it remains valid. Payment details are never sent to or stored by this app.

Sharing and imports

Exports, imported files, clipboard copies, and operating-system share actions happen only when you request them. Their handling then depends on the destination you choose.
